# Darkstar — Project Brief

**Event:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Abu Dhabi local event  
**Team:** Darkstar (solo; project lead)  
**Challenge:** Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

## Mission
Explore an interactive Mars navigation and expedition-planning experience grounded in NASA planetary data.

## Candidate capabilities
- Georeferenced Martian imagery and elevation
- Terrain-aware routes and elevation profiles
- Route comparisons with transparent assumptions
- Optional resource estimates and simulated hazards
- Scientific provenance and uncertainty disclosure

## Status
Research and planning only. No competition implementation has begun.

## Principles
1. NASA data must inform the actual decisions.
2. Calculations, units, and assumptions must be auditable.
3. Choose a well-covered Mars region rather than pretending to map all of Mars accurately.
4. Check official competition requirements before building.
